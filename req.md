Here is one example to run end to end: buying and installing 70 UPS units for base stations. Each section matches the requisition form and the activation steps. I've changed the price and dates at activation on purpose, so you also see the amber "changed from requisition" marking.

## 1. The requisition (raised by an IT requester)

**1. Description**
- **Title:** Supply, Installation & Commissioning of 10kVA UPS Units – Base Stations
- **Description:** Supply of 70 × 10kVA online double-conversion UPS units with external battery packs. Includes delivery to 14 base stations (Harare, Bulawayo, Mutare), installation, commissioning and acceptance testing.
- **Department:** Information Technology. **Contract type:** Supply & Services.

**2. Vendor**
| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Registered name | Volt Power Solutions (Pvt) Ltd |
| Trading name | VoltPower |
| Address | 14 Kelvin Road North, Graniteside, Harare |
| Email / phone | tenders@voltpower.co.zw · +263 242 751 900 |
| Representative | Ms T. Ndlovu, Sales Director |

**3. Justification**
> Existing UPS units at 14 base stations are over 8 years old and have failed 23 times in the last 12 months, causing 61 hours of site downtime. Replacement is in the 2026 capex plan (ref CAPEX-IT-2026-07). Three quotations were obtained; VoltPower was lowest compliant.

**4. Duration**
- **Start / end:** 1 Nov 2026 to 31 Oct 2027
- **Renewable:** Yes, for 1 year (this covers the maintenance)

**5. Value**
| Item | Amount |
|---|---|
| Currency | USD |
| Contract price | 45,000.00 |
| VAT (15.5%) | 6,975.00 |
| **Total** | **51,975.00** |

**6. Payment terms**
- **Payment terms text:** 30% advance on signing against an advance payment guarantee, 40% on delivery of all units, 30% on successful acceptance testing.
- **Down payment:** 15,592.50. **Balance:** 36,382.50, due after 30 days.
- **Advance payment guarantee required:** Yes

**7. Delivery terms**
- **Delivery period:** 12 weeks
- **Conditions:** DAP to the listed base stations. Supplier offloads and positions units.
- **Penalties:** 0.5% of the contract price per week of delay, capped at 10%.
- **Acceptance testing:** 4-hour load test and battery autonomy test per site, witnessed by Powertel Technical.

**8. Warranty:** 24 months. Maintenance required: Yes.

**9. Special issues:** Supplier to remove and dispose of the old units at no cost and provide disposal certificates.

**What happens next:**
1. The requester submits.
2. HOD (Information Technology) signs.
3. Finance Director signs.
4. Procurement Manager signs.
5. Company Secretary receives it and moves it to drafting.
6. The draft is agreed and every approver signs.
7. It lands in **Awaiting Activation**.

## 3. Testing it after activation

1. **Procurement Manager**, on Deliveries & Payments or the contract page, clicks **Confirm delivery** on deliverable 2. Use date 22 Jan 2027 and GRN-0193.
   - Payment 2 automatically gets the due date 22 Jan 2027.
   - Finance is notified that the payment is now due.
2. **Finance Director** clicks **Record payment** on payment 1. Use ref RTGS-88213 and amount 15,072.75.
   - The Payment plan card shows 15,072.75 paid.
   - The requester and their HOD are notified.
3. **Late delivery:** to see a late reminder, set deliverable 1's due date in the past before activating. It will show as overdue in red.
4. **Renewal:** because the contract is renewable, the renewal reminder starts 60 days before 14 Nov 2027. To test it straight away, temporarily set the end date about 50 days ahead. Then propose **Renew** to 14 Nov 2028 with the change "Annual maintenance fee USD 4,800".

To test the steps in the right roles, create these users first: an IT requester, the IT HOD, Finance Director, Procurement Manager and Company Secretary.
